## P7 — Viruses are part of us literally because we share part of the DNA with them [H→D]

His claim: viral code is literally part of us.
- Foothold [D]: human endogenous retroviruses (HERVs) are the remnants of ancient germ-cell infections by exogenous retroviruses and occupy up to 8% of the human genome. HERV-K integrated roughly 28 million years ago. The protein syncytin, derived from an ancient viral gene, is essential for placenta formation in mammals — without this viral gift, mammals as we know them might not exist.
- Honest line and distinction [D]: this holds for retroviruses, which integrate DNA into the germline. SARS-CoV-2 is a positive-sense RNA coronavirus; it does not integrate into human germline DNA as part of its life cycle. "We share DNA with viruses" is true of retroviral inheritance, not of SARS-CoV-2 specifically.
- Related prior thesis (his, Oct 16): viral DNA, human DNA, and other organisms' DNA could show how the systems are connected through people — footholds then: HERVs, pneumococcal mosaic genes, metagenomic sequencing.
- Verdict: HOLDS [H→D] — viruses are literally part of us via endogenous retroviruses, ~8% of our genome. The SARS-CoV-2-specific version does not follow; keep the general claim and the specific virus distinct.
- Simply put: About eight percent of your DNA is old virus code, built into us millions of years ago — including a viral gene that builds the placenta. That's literal. It just isn't this coronavirus's code.
