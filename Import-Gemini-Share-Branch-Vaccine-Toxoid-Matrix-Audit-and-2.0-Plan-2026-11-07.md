## Key updates and corrections [H, via Gemini]

- Adding PCV21 (Capvaxive) alongside PCV15 / PCV20 and PPSV23.
- Adding MenABCWY combinations (Penbraya and Penmenvy).
- Distinguishing CRM197 — a genetically modified, nontoxic diphtheria-toxin variant (Gly52Glu) — from formalin-detoxified diphtheria toxoid (DT) and tetanus toxoid (TT). This matches the standing rule: TT ≠ DT ≠ CRM197, never jump shared-sugar to human protection.
- Clarifying Hib PRP-T vs. PRP-OMP schedules.
- Distinguishing Vi-TT from experimental Vi-rEPA typhoid conjugates.
- Adding a 2026 maternal GBS6 phase 1/2 trial (216 pregnant participants) and preclinical *Cryptococcus* GXM conjugate work.
