# Medical research and continuous updates

A durable place for Douglas Roy Schoonover's ongoing medical-research
thoughts and updates, saved for research purposes.

New thoughts and updates land here first, as continuous entries, and feed
into the research work already built — serotype and disease grouping,
vaccine and toxoid work, and his manuscripts — rather than starting each
thought as a separate scattered note.

## How it works

- Each update is formed as a theory or postulate and tested under his
  standing method: six tracks kept separate (vaccines, viruses, diseases,
  illnesses, sicknesses, infections), 51% bar, tier labels
  [D] / [H] / [H→D] / [LOGIC] / [GAP] / [PARTIAL], old with new.
- Updates feed into existing work, not replace it: Serogroup Table,
  Serology Cross-Reference, Toxoid Table, Duck.ai multi-model
  assimilation, Shared Research Bundle, and his manuscripts
  (The Mycelial Transposition, Quantum Medicine, and others).
- These are creative research manuscripts and notes, not medical advice.
