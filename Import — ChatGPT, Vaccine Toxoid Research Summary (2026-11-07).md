## 2. Comprehensive audit — "Vaccine–Toxoid Comparative Matrix | November 5, 2026"
Two parallel tracks, reconciled per pathogen; lab mechanism ≠ clinical intervention. Track A scientific/biological, Track B medical/clinical. Initial finding: needs more than carrier correction — updated vaccine landscape, clearer distinction between antigen coverage, clinical protection, experimental promise. Pneumococcal: account for PCV21, not stop at PCV20. Meningococcal: account for MenABCWY.

Row 1 — Streptococcus pneumoniae — "Core mechanism validated / Formulation correction required"
Capsule = serotype-specific capsular polysaccharides; conjugation enables carrier peptides to helper T cells; class switching, affinity maturation, memory; serotypes not automatically cross-protected.
PCV7 7 serotypes CRM197; PCV13 13 CRM197; PCV15 15 CRM197; PCV20 20 CRM197; PCV21 21 CRM197; PPSV23 23 unconjugated. PCV21 is adult vaccine, with additional FDA-approved use in certain higher-risk younger populations; indication differs from PCV20. Correction: remove TT and DT from Prevnar carrier cell.

Row 2 — Neisseria meningitidis — "Strong biological and clinical foundation"
MenACWY = capsular polysaccharides; MenB = recombinant proteins (fHbp, NadA, NHBA, OMV) because group B capsule is similar to human neural cell adhesion molecules. US: MenACWY conjugates using CRM197 or TT depending on product; MenB recombinant; MenABCWY combinations. Penbraya = TT-conjugated MenACWY + recombinant fHbp; Penmenvy = MenACWY conjugates + recombinant MenB antigens + OMV. Original carrier description broadly correct but insufficiently product-specific.

Row 3 — Haemophilus influenzae type b — "Validated"
Target PRP, polyribosylribitol phosphate. PRP-T = tetanus toxoid; PRP-OMP = meningococcal outer membrane protein complex. CDC: three-dose primary for PRP-T, two-dose for PRP-OMP, followed by booster. Original should identify formulations explicitly.

Row 4 — Salmonella Typhi — "Mechanism validated / Commercial status needs qualification"
Vi capsular polysaccharide; Vi-rEPA = early conjugate, recombinant Pseudomonas aeruginosa exoprotein A; Vi-TT = tetanus toxoid, basis of established TCV products. 2026 CDC Yellow Book: TCVs licensed and WHO-prequalified internationally, but not currently licensed or available in the United States. Do not present Vi-rEPA and Vi-TT as equivalent commercial products.

Row 5 — Group B Streptococcus — "Human clinical research established / Not licensed"
Six-serotype platform Ia, Ib, II, III, IV, V; capsular polysaccharide conjugation to CRM197. 2026 randomized phase 1/2 maternal GBS6: 216 pregnant participants, 209 infants born. Robust antibody responses against all six serotypes; functional opsonophagocytic responses; maternal-to-infant placental transfer; similar adverse-event frequencies vs placebo; no demonstrated clinical efficacy against infant GBS disease in this small study. Supports continued development, not proven prevention in routine practice.

Row 6 — Diphtheria — "Established medical application"
Diphtheria toxoid = detoxified diphtheria toxin retaining antigenic properties; formalin changes structure; stimulates protective antitoxin antibodies; also a carrier. In DT, Td, DTaP, Tdap; objective is prevent toxin-mediated disease, not necessarily eliminate colonization. Correction: distinguish complete diphtheria toxoid from CRM197 — CRM197 is a genetically modified, nontoxic diphtheria toxin variant, not simply another name for formalin-detoxified diphtheria toxoid.

Row 7 — Tetanus — "Established medical application"
Tetanus toxoid = detoxified tetanus toxin; antibodies neutralize tetanospasmin; also a carrier. In DTaP, Tdap, Td, etc.; immunity is principally antitoxin immunity; vaccination does not reliably prevent organism entering a wound or forming spores. Correction: TT as "universal carrier" is too broad — widely used, not universal.

Row 8 — Cryptococcus neoformans / gattii — "Experimental research" — "arguably the most interesting cross-domain research direction"
Targets GXM glucuronoxylomannan (dominant), GalXM galactoxylomannan / glucuronoxylomannogalactan. Conjugation to TT, DT, CRM197 investigated; review reports protective effects in animal models. No licensed human cryptococcal vaccine; important in immunocompromised populations; animal antibodies ≠ human protection. Semisynthetic GXM decasaccharide conjugated to CRM197: antibodies in mice, survival 18 vs 15 days, p = 0.06, not conventionally significant. Correction: do not imply C. neoformans efficacy automatically applies to C. gattii.

## 3. Toxoid Bridge — cross-pathogen comparison
CRM197 — conjugate carrier, widely used; TT — toxoid vaccine and conjugate carrier; DT — toxoid vaccine and conjugate carrier; OMPC — protein carrier alternative; Recombinant proteins — direct antigen targets, important for MenB.
Mechanism: B cell recognizes polysaccharide, internalizes conjugate, processes carrier into peptides, presents on MHC class II, carrier-specific helper T cells assist, B cell class switches, affinity matures, forms memory. Carrier selection is a biological variable, not a manufacturing detail.
Exceptions: MenB molecular mimicry; PPSV23 unconjugated, no T-cell-dependent memory; Diphtheria/tetanus target toxin neutralization, not necessarily preventing infection; Cryptococcus experimental; GBS maternal transfer promising, efficacy unestablished.
