## Part 6 — Related organisms, species, and panels (not pneumococcal serotypes)

- **PN23M panel is NOT a genus panel** [D]: it is *S. pneumoniae* IgG to the 23 PPSV23 serotypes only. Missing from PN23M: ~70–80 pneumococcal serotypes (total known ~100, Oxford Maiden lab "100 different pneumococcal serotypes identified to date"; 2023 review counted 101), including PCV21-only 15A, 15C, 16F, 23A, 23B, 24F, 31, 35B and 6A, and every non-pneumococcal species.
- **More complete test?** For isolate identity, yes — Quellung (full antiserum panel), multiplex PCR (~30 serotypes/reaction), WGS serotyping (SeroBA "can identify 92 serotypes and two subtypes") — but those need a cultured isolate, not serum. **There is NO routine serum antibody panel covering all 100+ serotypes and NO single panel covering all Streptococcus species** [D].
- **Mitis relatives, closest first** [D]: *S. pseudopneumoniae* and *S. mitis* most closely related, then oralis, sanguinis, gordonii, parasanguinis, infantis, cristatus, peroris, australis, oligofermentans, sinensis; further out, other Streptococcus groups (anginosus, gallolyticus, mitis, mutans, pyogenes, salivarius). All Gram-positive cocci — same quadrant of the quad axis.
- **Group A vs B** [D]: both beta-haemolytic Gram-positive cocci, so haemolysis does not separate them. A = *S. pyogenes*, B = *S. agalactiae*; Lancefield antigen chemistry, bacitracin sensitive/PYR+ vs resistant/PYR−, CAMP−/hippurate− vs CAMP+/hippurate+, throat/skin vs GI/vaginal, classic diseases as in the Species file; definitive = Lancefield latex grouping. A and B are different species, not variants of one — split reflects niches, distinct cell-wall antigens, distinct virulence toolkits; in quad terms they sit in the same quadrant, so Lancefield carbohydrate is a third axis beyond shape × Gram.
- **Quad axis** [H→D]: shape × Gram = Gram-positive cocci, Gram-negative cocci, Gram-positive bacilli, Gram-negative bacilli — a framework always present even when a type is absent in a given sample. Pneumoniae is Gram-positive cocci.
- **"Serotype" for other streptococci** means Lancefield grouping / GBS capsular types Ia, Ib, II–IX — not pneumococcal serotypes, not in PN23M.
