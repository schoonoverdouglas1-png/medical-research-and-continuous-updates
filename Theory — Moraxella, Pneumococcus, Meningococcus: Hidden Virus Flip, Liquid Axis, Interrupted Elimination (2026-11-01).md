## P1 — New Moraxella and pneumococcus, with the virus as parallel [H]

**Simply put:** Moraxella, pneumococcus, and a virus live in the same nose-and-throat neighborhood, and they rise and fall together.

**Test, three ways:**

## P4 — Pneumococcus and meningococcal disease are part of both our immune system and the cure both [H]

**Test, three ways:**

3. "Cure" split: as prevention/immunity, HOLDS (carriage antibodies can block later invasion). As treatment of existing disease, BREAKS — carriage does not cure meningitis or pneumonia once invasive; that is treatment's job, and the same carrier strain can itself invade in a small minority.

**Verdict: HOLDS partial [H→D] — both immune system and reservoir, yes; "cure" only in the prevention sense, not the treatment sense.**
