## The proposal in 30 seconds
PN23M — *Streptococcus pneumoniae* IgG Antibodies, 23 Serotypes, serum, bead-based multiplex immunoassay [D] — is currently used as a retrospective audit of humoral response after pneumococcal vaccination [D]. SP23D proposes using it a second way: as a serial, real-time monitor of ongoing antigenic load, read at 6+ months after vaccination, when the vaccine-driven surge has cleared. Titers are not just memory. They are engagement. [H — Douglas's postulate]

## Two windows — HOLDS [D]
- **Original window, 4–6 weeks after vaccination:** antibody responses develop in ~75–85% of nonimmunocompromised adults and older children approximately 4–6 weeks following immunization with purified polysaccharide vaccines. [D — Mayo Clinic Laboratories, PN23M test definition]
- **SP23D window, 6+ months after vaccination:** [H] Douglas's proposal. No published SP23D window exists; the 6-month clearance claim is his postulate, not a documented interval. [GAP — no source found for a 6-month vaccine-surge clearance rule for PN23M]

## Two thresholds — TESTED, BOTH NEED REPAIR
### 1. Active illness cutoff ≥0.3 µg/mL per serotype — BREAKS as stated
- What the record actually says: WHO 0.35 µg/mL is the correlate of **protection** against invasive pneumococcal disease in infants [D]; serotype-specific correlates run 0.78–2.83 µg/mL for 1, 3, 7F, 19A, 19F, and 0.14–0.20 µg/mL for 6A, 6B, 18C, 23F [D]; Luminex equivalent for 19A is 0.12 µg/mL [D]. Quest's own report on Douglas's panel says ≥0.3–0.50 µg/mL suggests protection in healthy children, and ≥1.3 µg/mL may be more relevant after polysaccharide vaccines or for immunocompromised patients [D].
- SP23D inverts that: ≥0.3 = active illness, not protection. That inversion is [H], not [D]. It is testable, and it is the paper's central claim — it must be labelled as a postulate to be validated, not as a documented cutoff.
- His own panels, counted at ≥0.3: **Mayo Oct 29, 2025 — 11 of 23 serotypes ≥0.3**; **Quest Oct 26, 2026 — 17 of 23 serotypes ≥0.3**. At ≥1.0 (Mayo's normal mark): Mayo 1 of 23, Quest 8 of 23.

### 2. Systemic Load Index ≥9.7 µg/mL aggregate — BREAKS as an aggregate sum
- **Finding, three ways:** (1) Mayo's PNTOR total-IgG test definition gives reference values **≥9.7 mcg/mL** for **total** antipneumococcal IgG, with <9.7 indicating a poor vaccine response [D — Mayo PNTOR]. (2) That 9.7 belongs to a different assay — total IgG, not the sum of 23 serotype-specific IgGs. (3) Douglas's own panel sums do not produce 9.7: Mayo sum ≈ **6.9 µg/mL** (range ~6.8–7.0, two values <0.1), Quest sum **26.7 µg/mL** (his assumption 0.2 for each of six <0.3 values; range 25.5–27.24 if those six are 0–0.29).
- **Verdict:** 9.7 as a *sum-of-23* threshold is a category error, borrowed from the total-IgG test. It cannot stand as written. Keep 9.7 in the paper only as: "9.7 is the Mayo total-IgG reference value; whether a sum-of-23 index has any equivalent threshold is [GAP] — no derivation on file."
- Quest sum is driven by one outlier: **10A = 10.7 µg/mL** (Mayo 2025: 10A = 1.6, still the highest). A sum index that one serotype can dominate needs a per-serotype cap or a median, or it is not a load index at all. [LOGIC — Ember's addition, labelled as hers]

## Featured serotype 19A — PARTIAL
- **Bacterial side HOLDS [H→D]:** 19A–ST320 is a multidrug-resistant lineage, CC320 / GPSC1, resistant to penicillin, macrolides, cephalosporins, and increasingly carbapenems, via mosaic pbp1a / pbp2b / pbp2x [D — Lee et al., PLoS One 2025; Microorganisms 2026 review]. Post-PCV7 replacement driver [D].
- **Fungal side PARTIAL [PARTIAL]:** *Candida albicans* + *Streptococcus* spp. (mitis group, *S. oralis*, *S. gordonii*, *S. mutans*) form mucosal biofilms together [D — Morales & Hogan, PLoS Pathog 2010; Candida–streptococcal mucosal biofilm study, Mol Oral Microbiol 2015]. **No 19A-specific *Candida* co-existence study was found.** 19A + *Candida* in the same niche is Douglas's hypothesis built on that general co-localization, not a documented 19A finding. [GAP for 19A-specific]
- **His own 19A titers:** Mayo 2025 **0.3**, Quest 2026 **0.6** — both ≥0.3, both <1.0. 19A is not his highest serotype in either panel. 10A is.
- **Correction:** "methicillin-resistant" does **not** apply to pneumococcus. Methicillin resistance is *S. aureus* mecA. For pneumococcus the correct term is penicillin / macrolide / carbapenem non-susceptibility via mosaic PBP. [D — Ember's correction]

## No combined table — HOLDS [D, with a limit]
Meta searched and found no single published table combining fungal + bacterial elements with PN23M. Ember's search agrees: the official PN23M table is 23 serotypes only; resistance tables and fungal cross-reactivity literature are separate. The composite table in this paper is therefore **new work**, not a found table, and must be labelled as such.
