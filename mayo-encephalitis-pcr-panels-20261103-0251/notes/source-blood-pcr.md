# Blood / serum / plasma PCR — Mayo single-organism tests and true blood panels
- TIKLB Tick-Borne Panel, Molecular Detection, PCR, Blood — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/618311 — Whole Blood EDTA, 1 mL. Components BABPB Babesia spp., EPCRB Ehrlichia/Anaplasma (E. chaffeensis, E. ewingii/canis, E. muris eauclairensis, A. phagocytophilum), BMIPB Borrelia miyamotoi. Does NOT detect B. burgdorferi / B. mayonii. NY Yes.
- BRBST Tickborne Bacterial, PCR and Sequencing, Blood — https://mml.testcatalog.org/show/BRBST — Whole Blood EDTA, 1 mL, 16S PCR + NGS, NOT first-tier, only when routine testing negative; also detects Coxiella, Leptospira-like presentations. Rochester.
- WNV: WNVS serum https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/608436 (serum 0.5 mL, RT-PCR lineages 1+2, NY Yes, whole blood more sensitive up to 87% vs serum 26% per Mayo text); WNVBL whole blood EDTA; WNCSF CSF; WNVUR urine — all listed in Mosquito algorithm PDF.
- POWV: POWVB blood, POWVS serum, POWVC CSF https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/617485, POWVU urine; RT-PCR lineages 1+2, early infection, urine may stay positive >=7 days. POWVC NY Yes.
- HRTVS Heartland Virus RNA PCR Serum — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/download-setup?format=pdf&unit_code=620057 — serum 0.5 mL, NY Yes; >1 week symptoms may be PCR-negative, serology via CDC. HRTVC is CSF version.
- HSVPB Herpes Simplex Virus PCR Blood — https://microbiology.testcatalog.org/show/HSVPB — Whole Blood EDTA 1 mL, HSV-1/2, disseminated disease. Jacksonville duplicate LHSVB https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/802067 (Jacksonville-only, other clients use HSVPB).
- ENTP Enterovirus PCR Plasma — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/89893 — Plasma EDTA 1 mL, no serotyping.
- QHV6P HHV-6 A/B DNA Quant PCR Plasma — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/619925 — Plasma EDTA 1 mL, NY Yes.
- CMV: CMVQN plasma quant (ordering guidance on CMVPV https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/618968: for plasma order CMVQN); CMVPV Varies (body fluids incl CSF, NOT plasma). Jacksonville duplicate LCMV.
- VZV: VZVPV Varies (CSF, body fluid, swab, respiratory, tissue — blood NOT listed as a VZVPV specimen type in the definition excerpt); Jacksonville LVZV. No separate Mayo VZV blood PCR ID found — GAP, do not invent VZVPB.
- Parechovirus: no separate Mayo blood/plasma parechovirus PCR ID found in searches; parechovirus appears only inside CSFME — GAP.
- Bacterial meningitis organisms on blood: no Mayo blood multiplex for E. coli K1 / H. influenzae / Listeria / N. meningitidis / S. agalactiae / S. pneumoniae; workup is blood culture per algorithm + BRBPS/BRBST broad-range sequencing in selected cases (see below).
- FLARP Free-Living Amebae PCR Varies — https://kootenaihealth.testcatalog.org/show/FLARP — CSF 0.5 mL or tissue (brain/skin/lung); Acanthamoeba, Naegleria fowleri, Balamuthia mandrillaris; NY Yes. NOT blood.
- MSCSF Bacteria/Virus/Fungus/Parasite Metagenomic Sequencing, Spinal Fluid — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/download-setup?format=pdf&unit_code=620941 — CSF 2 mL, NGS not PCR panel, lumbar-puncture CSF only, shunt/ventricular not acceptable, NY Yes.
- KPLA/KDPLA Karius Spectrum Plasma — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/download-setup?format=pdf&unit_code=623289 — Plasma mcfDNA metagenomic sequencing, >1000 organisms, performed by Karius Laboratory (direct ship KDPLA) / via Mayo KPLA, NOT a Mayo PCR panel and NOT meningitis/encephalitis-specific.
