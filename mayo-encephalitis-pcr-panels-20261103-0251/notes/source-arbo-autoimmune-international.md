# ARBO, arbovirus antibody panels, autoimmune, international, CDC routing
- ARBO: NO Mayo test ID "ARBO" found. Closest Mayo IDs: ARBOP and ABOPC (below). Local client codes differ, e.g. Children's Minnesota ARBS for blood arbovirus panel referred to Mayo ARBOP (https://www.childrensmn.org/References/Lab/serology/arbovirus-antibody-panel-blood.pdf) — do not confuse a client EHR code with a Mayo ID.
- ARBOP Arbovirus Antibody Panel, IgG and IgM, Serum — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/83267 — Serum 0.5 mL, IFA (NOT PCR), California (La Crosse), Eastern equine, St. Louis, Western equine; components CAVP, EEEP, STLP, WEEP. NY Yes.
- ABOPC Arbovirus Antibody Panel, IgG and IgM, Spinal Fluid — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/83897 — CSF, IFA, same 4 viruses (CAVPC, EEPC, STLPC, WEEPC). NY: NOT available for specimens originating in New York.
- Mosquito-borne algorithm — https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/n1/96e99366cea7b0de/uploads/2025/05/Mosquito-borne_Disease_Laboratory_Testing.pdf (Mar 2024): US exposure -> ARBOP/ABOPC + individual panels; outside-US exposure -> CHIKV/CHIKS/CHIKC, DENGM/DENVP/DNSAG/DENGS/DENGC, VZIKM/VZIKU/VZIKS; any region -> WNS/WNC + WNVBL/WNVUR/WNVS/WNCSF. Filarial suspected: contact local public health or CDC for Bourbon virus, Jamestown Canyon virus options. Same branching, not a separate country catalog.
- Autoimmune — ANTIBODY, not PCR: ENS2 Encephalopathy, Autoimmune/Paraneoplastic Evaluation, Serum — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/92116 — serum, IFA/CBA/ELISA/RIA/Western/immunoblot; antibodies incl AMPA-R, amphiphysin, AGNA-1, ANNA-1/2/3, CASPR2, CRMP-5, DPPX, GABA-A/B, GAD65, IgLON5, KLHL11, LGI1, Ma2, mGluR1/5, neurochondrin, NMDA-R, PDE10A, PCA-1/2/Tr, septin-7, TRIM46, AK5. NY Yes.
- ENC2 same evaluation, Spinal Fluid — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/download-setup?format=pdf&unit_code=92117 — CSF, adult >=18; <18 auto-reordered as PCDEC Pediatric Autoimmune Encephalopathy/CNS Disorder Evaluation, Spinal Fluid; serum counterpart PCDES. Mayo guidance: send BOTH serum and CSF for encephalopathy (NMDA/GFAP/NIF better in CSF; LGI1/CASPR2 better in serum) — https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/autoimmune-testing-education/
- International / same catalog: Mayo Specialty Testing page says "testing used to diagnose and treat Mayo Clinic patients is available to patients around the world through Mayo Clinic Laboratories" — https://test.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/specialty-testing ; Global Capabilities — https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/international/global-capabilities/ — open-access catalog updated daily, MayoLINK in 8 languages, individualized global logistics, specimens from around the world to Rochester. No country-specific Mayo encephalitis/meningitis PCR panel found. Practical limit is specimen stability (e.g. CSFME refrigerated 7 days) + import/customs, not a different organism list.
- Jacksonville vs Rochester duplicates (within-US, not country): LHSV/LHSVB, LCMV, LVZV, LHSVZ are Jacksonville-only; other clients use Rochester HSVPV/HSVPB, CMVPV, VZVPV.
- Non-Mayo on-site panels abroad (explains "different panels under similar names"): BioFire FilmArray ME = Mayo CSFME 14 targets, CSF only. QIAstat-Dx ME (CE-IVD, Europe 2022) = 15 targets: adds Streptococcus pyogenes and Mycoplasma pneumoniae, does NOT include CMV per comparative tables — https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/spectrum.05144-22 and https://www2.mdpi.com/2075-4418/14/8/802 — secondary literature, not a Mayo test. Neither syndromic panel is validated for blood.
- CDC / public health: Heartland serology via CDC (HRTVS definition); Bourbon/Jamestown Canyon via local PH/CDC (mosquito PDF); CSFME positives may need extra testing per local PH requirements (CSFME page). No Mayo PCR panel for Japanese encephalitis or tick-borne encephalitis virus found — those are outside the Mayo mosquito algorithm's US/outside-US lists and are a GAP for a named Mayo ID.
