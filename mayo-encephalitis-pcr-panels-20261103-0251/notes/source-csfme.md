# CSFME — Meningitis/Encephalitis Pathogen Panel, PCR, Spinal Fluid
URL: https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/622764 — read via index, page crawled 2026-11-02 16:29 UTC
- Specimen Type: CSF only. Sterile vial, 1 mL, refrigerated within 7 days, frozen NOT acceptable, shunt fluid rejected.
- Method: Multiplex PCR (FilmArray). 14 targets: E. coli K1, H. influenzae, L. monocytogenes, N. meningitidis (encapsulated only), S. agalactiae, S. pneumoniae, CMV, enterovirus, HSV-1, HSV-2, HHV-6, human parechovirus, VZV, Cryptococcus neoformans/gattii.
- NY State Available: No.
- Ordering guidance: not usually concomitantly order CMVPV, LENT, HSVC, VZVPV. Local public-health requirements may require additional testing on positives; retain aliquot.
- Algorithm PDF: https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/en/articles/resources/-/media/it-mmfiles/special%20instructions/a/a/5/meningitis-encephalitis_panel_algorithm.pdf — acute <8 days, lumbar puncture first, blood cultures listed alongside CSF workup (not as PCR panel substitute).
