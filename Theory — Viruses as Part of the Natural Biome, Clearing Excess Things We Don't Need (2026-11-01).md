## Tests

### P1 — Viruses as part of the natural biome
**HOLDS [D].**

The "biome" here is read as the virome / biosphere's viral component, and on that reading the claim is documented, not speculative:
- Every ecosystem studied has a virome: oceans, soils, guts, skin, airways — viruses are present wherever their hosts are, in numbers that dwarf their hosts (in the oceans, an estimated ~10³⁰ virus particles, the most abundant biological entities on Earth) [D, general].
- In the human body, the virome includes bacteriophages of the gut and skin bacteria, eukaryotic viruses (many latent or commensal), and endogenous viral sequences already in our DNA (HERVs, ~8% — tonight's earlier file). A human without a virome is not a documented state; a human *with* one is the normal state.
- This connects directly to his earlier work: the Oct 31 Nesting-Doll / Scrubber Model file, where P9 already stated "viruses as clearing mechanism more than problem" [H doctrine, with documented instances including Sputnik virophage protecting amoebae]. Tonight's dictation is that doctrine restated in biome language.

### P2 — Viruses eat up extra excess things we don't need
**BREAKS literally, HOLDS as a clearing mechanism — and the distinction is the same one from his earlier Fingers/Clearing file.**

- Breaks literally: viruses do not eat. They have no metabolism, no mouth, no digestive system — a point already tested in his Nov 1 Fingers/Clearing theory ("viruses do not eat"). Eating, in the animal sense, is not something a virus does.
- Holds as clearing: what viruses *do* is infect and, in lytic infections, lyse their hosts — and that lysis removes excess host populations and recycles their contents:
  - **In ecosystems — the viral shunt [D, general]:** marine viruses lyse bacteria and phytoplankton daily, releasing their carbon, nitrogen, and other nutrients back into the water for other organisms to use. Excess bacterial biomass is, in effect, eaten up — not by a mouth, but by lysis and recycling.
  - **In the human gut — phage control [D, general]:** bacteriophages prey on gut bacteria and help hold bacterial populations in check; a phage bloom can reduce an overgrown bacterial strain, which is population control by virus.
  - **In fungi — mycovirus attenuation [D, from tonight's files]:** a mycovirus can weaken its fungal host, reducing excess fungal growth or virulence.
  - **In the body — immune clearance [H→D]:** the earlier Virus Encephalopathy file tested immune clearance (phagocytosis, neutralization, T-cell killing) as a pathway by which virus and virus-infected cells are removed. The virus itself is not the eater there; the immune system is — but the virus's presence can trigger the clearing.

**What "extra excess things we don't need" can and cannot mean:**
- Can mean: overgrown bacterial populations, excess host cells that are infected or abnormal (in the oncolytic sense), and biomass that, once lysed, is recycled rather than accumulated.
- Cannot mean, on the current record: viruses selectively identifying what *we* (humans, consciously) do not need and removing only that. Viruses have no intent and no knowledge of human need; they infect what their receptors allow, and the "excess" that gets cleared is whatever host population is abundant and susceptible — which is often, but not always, what we would want cleared. A virus that lyses a beneficial gut bacterium is clearing something we *do* need. Selectivity is by receptor and abundance, not by human benefit.

## Combined statement (his thought + tested thought)

His biome picture holds: viruses are not outsiders to nature, they are part of its standing population, in oceans, soils, guts, and genomes alike — and their lytic action genuinely clears excess host biomass and recycles it, which is the closest nature comes to "eating up the extra." The tested correction is only about the verb: viruses don't eat, they infect and lyse, and they clear what is abundant and susceptible, not what we consciously judge unneeded. Keep "clearing" and the theory survives every test here; keep "eating" literally and it breaks on the first one — which is why his own earlier Clearing file already made that correction, and tonight's dictation is best read as that file's doctrine in new words.
