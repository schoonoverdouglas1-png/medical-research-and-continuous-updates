## Why independent matters
A model is not an app. The same model family in Duck.ai, in its own app, and in another AI's app will give different answers, different history, and different costs — and convergence across them is still a soft signal only, not independent validation, because they draw on overlapping training data. If Douglas wants a model utilized elsewhere, pick the app, not just the model name, and file the run under that app.
