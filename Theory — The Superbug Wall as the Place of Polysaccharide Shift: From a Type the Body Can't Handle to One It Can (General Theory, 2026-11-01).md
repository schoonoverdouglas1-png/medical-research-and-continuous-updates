## P1 — The superbug wall is where the polysaccharide shift occurs [H→D]

**Claim:** the wall is the place of the polysaccharide shift.

**Tested:**
1. *Capsule = polysaccharide [D]:* pneumococcal conjugate vaccines target the capsular polysaccharide (CPS) — a key virulence factor that enables immune evasion — and the structural diversity of CPS allows differentiation into more than 100 serotypes. Serotype IS capsular polysaccharide identity.
2. *Shift = capsular switching [D]:* capsular switching allows preexisting clones expressing vaccine serotypes to escape vaccine-induced immunity by acquisition of capsular genes from pneumococci of a nonvaccine serotype. The shift is a gene acquisition that changes the wall's polysaccharide.
3. *The wall is where dealing happens [D]:* opsonophagocytic killing — the immune system's handling of the pneumococcus — acts on the capsule; switched variants show lower susceptibility to opsonophagocytic killing. The encounter, and the evasion, both happen at the wall.

**Verdict:** HOLDS [H→D] — the wall is not a metaphor for the shift; it is the shift's location. The polysaccharide sits on the wall, the switch rewrites the wall, and the immune system meets the wall first.
- Simply put: The capsule is the wall's coat, made of sugar chains. Change the coat and you've changed the wall — and that's where the immune system shakes hands with the germ, or fails to.

**Verdict:** BREAKS as the general direction; HOLDS as his cure mechanism and in specific cases (fitness-cost switches, cross-reactive coverage). The honest grade is: his direction is the exception and the cure case, not the rule — and that is precisely why it matters as a theory of cure rather than as a description of usual switching.
- Simply put: Usually the germ switches coats to dodge what your body already knows. Your theory says the shift can run the other way — landing on a coat your body can handle.
