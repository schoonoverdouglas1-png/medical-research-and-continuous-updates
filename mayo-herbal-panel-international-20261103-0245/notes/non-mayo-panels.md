# Non-Mayo arbovirus panels — different tests, not Mayo renames — index, read 2026-11-02
- NZ Canterbury Health Laboratories, Arbovirus PCR, code 9273 / PARB: https://www.chl.co.nz/test/arbovirus-pcr/ — panel = Chikungunya, Dengue, Zika only (PCR).
- Australia PathWest, Arbovirus Serology SEARB (serum) / SEARC (CSF): https://pathwesttd.health.wa.gov.au/testdirectory/testdetail.aspx?TestID=1792 — selection by clinical/travel history: flaviviruses dengue, Zika, Japanese encephalitis, Murray Valley encephalitis, Kunjin/West Nile, yellow fever; alphaviruses chikungunya, Ross River, Barmah Forest; IgM+IgG per selected virus.
- US NY Wadsworth (non-Mayo, public health): https://wadsworth.org/node/3278/printable/print — Travel-Associated Arbovirus Panel 6150 PCR = Dengue, Chikungunya, Oropouche, Zika; Arbovirus Summer Panel 5470 (serum/urine/whole blood); Viral Encephalitis Panel - Summer 686 (CSF); individual Dengue 2510, Chikungunya 2630, Oropouche 6210, Yellow Fever 4491, Zika 3031.
- Maryland (non-Mayo): http://health.maryland.gov/laboratories/Pages/Arbovirus-Serology.aspx — Arbovirus Endemic Panel = WNV, SLE, EEE IgM (serum/CSF), La Crosse by history; Arbovirus Travel-Associated Panel = DENV, CHIKV, ZIKV IgM.
- Minnesota MDH (non-Mayo): https://forms.web.health.state.mn.us/idl/test/861 (serum, ARBO) and /860 (CSF, ARBOCSF) — panel may change by season, may include West Nile, Powassan, Jamestown Canyon, EEE, WEE, California-group, SLE. WARNING: same generic name/code stem "ARBO" as Mayo shorthand, but broader/different composition.
- Virginia DCLS Human Arbo Viral Panel (non-Mayo): https://dgs.virginia.gov/division-of-consolidated-laboratory-services/resources/test-catalog/human-arbo-viral-panel — IgM MIA/ELISA + IgG ELISA, serum/CSF, approval required.
- UK RIPL geographic panels (non-Mayo): https://www.gov.uk/guidance/zika-virus-sample-testing-advice — for acutely unwell returning travellers, RIPL performs Zika testing as part of a geographic panel of PCR+serology without Zika having to be specifically requested; panel chosen by region/symptoms/travel.
- CDC/APHL Arbovirus Reference Center menu Sept 2026 (non-Mayo): https://aphl.org/docs/default-source/infectious-diseases-documents/vector-borne-diseases/arbovirusreferencecenter_testmenus.pdf?sfvrsn=75ba9d05_2 — individual-virus menu, Travel Panel PCR 6150, Summer Panel 5470, JCV IgG IFA 606, PRNT 3170.
- Same Mayo test, different local codes (NOT different panels): UW Medicine Lab 646 https://testguide.labmed.uw.edu/view/646?tabs=yes ; Children's Minnesota ARBS; CentraCare LAB0298463 https://centracarelaboratoryservices.testcatalog.org/show/LAB0298463 ; WakeMed LAB3972 for ABOPC; HealthLab 2192910. All cite Mayo ARBOP/ABOPC, same 4 viruses, IFA, Rochester performing lab.
- Assay kits, not clinical panels: bioMerieux VIDAS Dengue panel (NS1, IgM, IgG) + Chikungunya panel (IgM, IgG) https://www.biomerieux.com/corp/en/our-offer/clinical-products/vidas-arboviruses-panel.html ; EUROIMMUN Arbovirus Fever Mosaic 2 (Zika, chikungunya, dengue 1-4) — PDF via eslbioscience.
- Yellow fever / Japanese encephalitis at Mayo: NO Mayo test ID found in catalog searches. They appear only as cross-reactivity/specificity organisms in Mayo Powassan/West Nile PCR pages, and CDC guidance says JE confirmatory testing only at CDC + few specialized labs, via state health department: https://www.cdc.gov/japanese-encephalitis/hcp/clinical-diagnosis/index.html
