# Mayo Clinic ARBO (arbovirus) panels internationally — research report

## Scope correction — read first
- **The herbal angle is DROPPED entirely.** Per the correction received 2026-11-02: the user said **ARBO, not herbal, each time; "herbal" was a transcription error.**
- This report is only about **ARBO = arbovirus** panel tests from Mayo Clinic Laboratories, in other countries vs the United States.
- **No herbal findings are included**, and no herbal panel is claimed or denied here — that question is out of scope after the correction.

## Summary
- **The user's US baseline is confirmed against Mayo's catalog.** Mayo's serum ARBO panel is exactly 8 results: 4 viruses × IgG + IgM — California (La Crosse), Eastern equine, St. Louis, Western equine. **There is no separate Jamestown Canyon analyte and no coronavirus analyte in it.** The CSF version has the same 4 viruses.
- **No country-specific Mayo ARBO panel was found.** Mayo Clinic Laboratories publishes **one** open-access test catalog (updated daily), performs ARBOP/ABOPC in Rochester, and states its protocols are applied **uniformly regardless of geographic origin**. International clients ship specimens to Rochester under one international shipping guide.
- **What changes internationally / by exposure is test *selection*, not the ARBO panel itself.** Mayo's mosquito-borne algorithm branches on exposure: US exposure → ARBOP/ABOPC; exposure outside the US → *add* separate chikungunya, dengue and Zika tests; any region → add West Nile tests. Powassan, Heartland, Jamestown Canyon and Bourbon virus are routed to CDC / public-health options in that algorithm, not added to ARBOP.
- **Mayo does offer separate, individually orderable tests for dengue, Zika, chikungunya and West Nile** (and Powassan) — but **no combined "international ARBO panel"** bundling those into ARBOP was found, and **no Mayo yellow-fever or Japanese-encephalitis test ID was found** in catalog searches (see Gaps).
- **Other countries do use the same Mayo test under different local codes/names** (client-lab relistings), **and** they also use **genuinely different local panels** with different compositions. The two must not be conflated: a generic name like "Arbovirus panel / ARBO" does **not** guarantee Mayo's 4-virus composition — e.g. Minnesota's public-health "ARBO" serum panel can include West Nile, Powassan and Jamestown Canyon, which Mayo's ARBOP does not.
- Confidence: **high** for the ARBOP/ABOPC composition and the single-catalog finding (primary Mayo catalog pages); **medium** for the absence of a country-specific Mayo panel and of Mayo yellow-fever/JE tests (absence from searched catalog pages, not a Mayo statement of non-existence); **high** that non-Mayo panels below differ, as their own pages list different analytes.

## Findings

### 1. The US Mayo ARBO panel — verified against the user's description

| Mayo test | Exact catalog name | ID | Specimen | Method | Analytes (each IgG + IgM = 8 results) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ARBOP | Arbovirus Antibody Panel, IgG and IgM, Serum | ARBOP | Serum, 0.5 mL (min 0.15 mL) | Immunofluorescence Assay (IFA) | CAVP — Calif Virus (LaCrosse); EEEP — East Equine Enceph; STLP — St. Louis Enceph; WEEP — West Equine Enceph |
| ABOPC | Arbovirus Antibody Panel, IgG and IgM, Spinal Fluid | ABOPC | Spinal fluid / CSF, 0.7 mL | IFA | CAVPC — Calif (LaCrosse); EEPC — East Equine; STLPC — St. Louis; WEEPC — West Equine |

- Source for both: Mayo catalog pages https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/83267 (ARBOP) and https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/83897 (ABOPC), read 2026-11-02 via index/catalog fetch (see Gaps on live-browser failure).
- ARBOP "Useful For": "Aiding the diagnosis of arboviral encephalitis due to California (La Crosse), St. Louis, Eastern equine, or Western equine encephalitis viruses" — no other virus named.
- Reference values: serum <1:10 for IgG and IgM for all four viruses, all ages; CSF <1:1 for IgG and IgM for all four.
- Performing laboratory for both: Mayo Clinic Laboratories – Rochester (ARBOP: Rochester Superior Drive). ARBOP is NY State Available = Yes; ABOPC is NY State Available = No — "not available for specimens originating in New York." That is a US *state* restriction, not a country-specific panel.
- Profile table lists each component as Always Performed = Yes, Available Separately = No within the panel. Mayo's own aliases for the panel include "Arbovirus Serology", "Central Nervous System Screen", "Alphavirus (old Arbovirus, group A)" and "Flavivirus (old Arbovirus, group B)".
- **Jamestown Canyon: confirmed absent as a separate analyte.** The ARBOP/ABOPC profile lists only the four components above. Jamestown Canyon appears in Mayo's mosquito-borne algorithm only as a virus to consider for CDC / public-health testing, not as an ARBOP component. Note on cross-reactivity (not a separate analyte): La Crosse is a California-serogroup virus, as is Jamestown Canyon, and Mayo's page warns of cross-reactivity in general (dengue vs St. Louis antigens; Eastern vs Western equine, typically ≥8-fold difference for the infecting virus) — but it does **not** state that a positive California (La Crosse) result equals a Jamestown Canyon result. Do not treat it as one.
- **Coronavirus: confirmed absent.** No coronavirus / SARS-CoV-2 component appears in either profile, and coronavirus is not an arbovirus and does not appear in Mayo's mosquito-borne testing menu. The user's "no coronavirus analyte" description is consistent with the catalog.

### 2. Is Mayo's catalog the same worldwide, or country-specific?
**Answer: one catalog, worldwide — no country-specific ARBO panel found.**
- Mayo's global page describes "our open-access test catalog, which is updated daily" and MayoLINK ordering "available in eight languages" — i.e. translation/localisation of ordering, not a different test menu. Source: https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/international/global-capabilities/?pg=13
- Mayo's commercial-laboratory page: "we've developed robust logistics and testing protocols applied uniformly for all specimens received, no matter their geographic origin. Whether you send us one test order or thousands, each sample receives the same treatment" and partners have "access to Mayo Clinic Laboratories' full menu." Source: https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/commercial-laboratories/?pg=2
- The international shipping guide is a *shipping* guide, not a separate catalog: it tells international clients to "check the specimen requirements in the test catalog" (the same catalog) and describes reference services to "more than 3,400 hospitals and laboratories in more than 60 countries worldwide." Source: https://test.mayocliniclabs.com/-/media/it-mmfiles/Special-Instructions/D/1/F/international-shipping-guide
- Mayo's specialty-testing page: "the testing used to diagnose and treat Mayo Clinic patients is available to patients around the world through Mayo Clinic Laboratories." Source (index): https://test.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/specialty-testing
- **Distinguish the entities:**
  - *Mayo Clinic Laboratories* = the US reference laboratory in Rochester that performs ARBOP/ABOPC. This is the panel in question.
  - *Mayo Clinic Healthcare, London* = a clinical clinic (15 Portland Place), not a separate laboratory catalog; secondary UK sources describe London patients as having access to Mayo Clinic Laboratories testing. No London-specific ARBO panel was found.
  - *Mayo representative offices* (e.g. Caribbean offices) coordinate appointments/travel for US/London care and provide no clinical care or testing themselves.
  - *Local laboratories abroad* that send specimens to Mayo (e.g. a Thai hospital listing a Mayo send-out) are ordering the Rochester test, not a local Mayo variant.
- Practical country differences are **logistical/regulatory, not compositional**: specimen-export rules, customs/IATA packaging, and state-level availability (NY for ABOPC) can block or reroute a specimen, and a secondary magazine account notes some countries do not allow specimens to leave the country at all (China given as an example), in which case Mayo partners locally. That is not a different ARBO panel.
- Gap: Mayo does not publish, on the pages found, an explicit statement "there is no country-specific catalog." The conclusion rests on the single daily-updated catalog + uniform-protocol statements + absence of any country-specific ARBO listing in searches.

### 3. Mayo's regional / exposure-based arbovirus testing — same catalog, different selection
Mayo does **not** fold travel-region viruses into ARBOP. Instead its mosquito-borne algorithm (Mayo, https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/n1/96e99366cea7b0de/uploads/2025/05/Mosquito-borne_Disease_Laboratory_Testing.pdf and mosquito-borne menu https://news.mayocliniclabs.com/infectious-disease/vector-borne-diseases/mosquito-borne-disease/) directs:

| Exposure | Mayo tests to consider (separate orders, not part of ARBOP) |
|---|---|
| Mosquito exposure in the US | ARBOP (serum) and/or ABOPC (CSF); single-virus panels CAVP/CAVPC, EEEP/EEPC, STLP/STLPC, WEEP/WEEPC |
| Mosquito exposure outside the US | CHIKV — Chikungunya IgM and IgG, Antibody, Serum; CHIKS — Chikungunya Virus, PCR, Molecular Detection, Serum; CHIKC — same, Spinal Fluid; DENGM — Dengue Virus Antibody, IgG and IgM, Serum; DENVP — Dengue Virus Antibody/Antigen Panel, Serum (IgG + IgM + NS1 antigen); DNSAG — Dengue Virus NS1 Antigen, Serum; DENGS — Dengue Virus, Molecular Detection, PCR, Serum; DENGC — same, Spinal Fluid; VZIKM — Zika Virus, IgM Antibody Capture ELISA, Serum; VZIKS — Zika Virus, PCR, Molecular Detection, Serum; VZIKU — Zika Virus, PCR, Molecular Detection, Random, Urine |
| Any region | WNS — West Nile Virus Antibody, IgG and IgM, Serum; WNC — West Nile Virus Antibody, IgG and IgM, Spinal Fluid; plus West Nile PCR: WNVS (serum), WNVBL (blood), WNVUR (urine), WNCSF (spinal fluid) |
| Powassan / Heartland / Jamestown Canyon / Bourbon suspected | Algorithm points to CDC / public-health testing options — **not** a Mayo panel. Mayo does separately offer Powassan tests: POWV (IgM ELISA, serum), POWVB (PCR, blood), POWVS (PCR, serum), POWVU (PCR, random urine) — tick-borne, listed under tick-borne rather than mosquito-borne algorithms. |

- Dengue detail (Mayo): DENGM, https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/83865 — ELISA, serum 0.5 mL, components DENG (IgG) + DENM (IgM) + DNABI interpretation. DENVP adds DENS1 (NS1 antigen), serum 1 mL.
- Chikungunya detail (Mayo): CHIKS, https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/603833 — real-time RT-PCR, serum, for acute phase (ideally <7 days); Mayo explicitly recommends concurrent dengue and Zika RNA/IgM testing because of overlapping syndromes.
- Zika detail (Mayo): VZIKS, https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/Overview/619432 — real-time RT-PCR, serum.
- **Yellow fever — no Mayo test found.** Searches of mayocliniclabs.com for a yellow-fever test returned no Mayo test ID; yellow fever appears in Mayo pages only as a cross-reactivity / specificity organism (e.g. in Powassan and West Nile PCR specificity panels). Do not assume Mayo offers it; CDC/state public-health referral is the route described for yellow-fever serology/neutralisation in the literature found.
- **Japanese encephalitis — no Mayo test found.** Same pattern: JE appears only as a specificity/cross-reactivity organism in Mayo West Nile/Powassan PCR pages. CDC guidance (https://www.cdc.gov/japanese-encephalitis/hcp/clinical-diagnosis/index.html) says JE diagnosis is serum/CSF IgM, confirmatory neutralising-antibody testing is "only available at CDC and a few specialized reference laboratories," and specimens go via state/local health departments to CDC's Arbovirus Diagnostic Laboratory. That is a non-Mayo route, not a Mayo panel.
- **No Mayo "international ARBO panel" bundling dengue + Zika + chikungunya + yellow fever + JE was found.** If a foreign client orders those viruses from Mayo, they are separate test IDs, as above.

### 4. Same Mayo test under different names/codes vs different tests entirely
**A. Same Mayo test, different local name/code — YES, common (US client labs; the pattern applies internationally to any Mayo send-out client).**
These all cite Mayo ARBOP/ABOPC, same 4 viruses, same IFA, Rochester performing lab — only the ordering system's code/name differs:

- **Assay kits are not panels from a lab:** bioMérieux VIDAS® Dengue Panel (NS1 Ag + anti-Dengue IgM + IgG) and VIDAS® Chikungunya Panel (IgM + IgG) — https://www.biomerieux.com/corp/en/our-offer/clinical-products/vidas-arboviruses-panel.html — and EUROIMMUN Arbovirus Fever Mosaic 2 (Zika, chikungunya, dengue 1–4 IFA) are commercial reagents used by local labs abroad, not Mayo tests and not equivalent to ARBOP.
- **Practical rule for the user:** to tell "same Mayo test, new code" from "different test," check (1) performing laboratory = Mayo Clinic Laboratories in Rochester, (2) Mayo Test ID ARBOP/ABOPC, (3) method IFA, and (4) the 4-virus list. If any of those differs (e.g. PCR, or dengue/Zika/Ross River in the list, or a public-health lab as performer), it is a different test even if it is called an "arbovirus panel."

## Could not verify / gaps
1. **Live-browser verification failed.** Two attempts to open Mayo's ARBOP page in the research window returned `page_gone` with no tabs. All Mayo facts above are therefore from **index/catalog fetches** (Mayo pages crawled 2026-10-28 to 2026-10-30, plus the mosquito algorithm PDF), not a live-window read. The catalog text is primary-source and internally consistent across Mayo and multiple client-lab relistings, but a live re-check before ordering is prudent.
2. **No explicit Mayo statement "we have no country-specific catalog" was found.** The single-catalog conclusion is inferred from the daily-updated open catalog, uniform-protocol statements, and absence of any country-specific ARBO listing — it is not a quoted Mayo denial.
3. **Yellow fever and Japanese encephalitis at Mayo: not found, not proven absent.** No Mayo test ID for either was returned by catalog searches; they appear only as cross-reactivity/specificity organisms. Mayo's referred-tests list was not fully searchable in this session, so a referral route via Mayo cannot be ruled out — but no such Mayo ID is cited here.
4. **No country-by-country Mayo price/availability table exists in the sources found.** Fees are account-specific ("contact your U.S. or International Regional Manager") on Mayo test pages. Availability for a specific foreign country (e.g. whether a specimen may legally leave that country) must be checked with Mayo's global team / the country's export rules at order time.
5. **Jamestown Canyon cross-reactivity nuance unverified:** whether a California-serogroup (La Crosse) IFA result in ARBOP would cross-react with Jamestown Canyon antigen is not stated on Mayo's ARBOP page (which discusses dengue and EEE/WEE cross-reactivity only). Do not infer a JCV result from ARBOP; JCV-specific testing is via public-health/CDC routes above.
6. **Herbal angle:** intentionally excluded after the correction; nothing in this report addresses herbal panels.
