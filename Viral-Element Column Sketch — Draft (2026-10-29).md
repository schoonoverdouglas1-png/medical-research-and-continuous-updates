## The definition problem, kept open (from P14)

P14's verdict depends entirely on what "viral element" means. Four candidate meanings, different verdicts each — the sketch uses meaning (b) only, because that is the one with documented rows:

- (a) A phage/mycovirus that infects the organism — widespread, documented.
- (b) A phage-encoded toxin gene — documented for diphtheria, cholera, Shiga.
- (c) A co-infecting pathogenic virus — documented for some diseases, not all.
- (d) An endogenous viral sequence — built into us, documented.

Under (b), the column is strong for 3 rows and GAP for the rest. Under (c) as a universal, P14 breaks — that reading is not used here.

That emptiness is the research program, not a failure of the column.
