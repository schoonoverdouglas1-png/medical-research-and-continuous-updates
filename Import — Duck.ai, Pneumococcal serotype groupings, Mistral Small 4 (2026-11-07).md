## 3. Molecular vs antigenic vs vaccine commonality
- Molecular: genetic differences (e.g. cps locus variations) do not always predict antigenic similarity.
- Antigenic: capsular polysaccharides determine serotype (e.g. 6A vs 6B differ in immunogenicity).
- Vaccine Commonality: some serotypes grouped (e.g. 6A/6B in PCV7) despite antigenic differences.
Cross-reactivity: 6A vs 6B — PCV7 includes 6B not 6A (6A in PCV13); 19A vs 19F — PCV7 includes 19F not 19A (19A in PCV13); 22F vs 22A — only 22F in PCV15/20/21.

## 4. What was not found
- Cross-protection data: some serotypes may induce partial immunity to others (e.g. 6A vs 6B), but not guaranteed.
- Serotype replacement: emerging non-vaccine serotypes (e.g. 15A, 23A) post-vaccine introduction.
- Disease-specific serotype shifts: some serotypes (e.g. 3) less immunogenic in vaccines.
